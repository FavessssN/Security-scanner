Asset Inventory and Classification Guide
======================================

What is Asset Inventory?
-----------------------
Think of asset inventory as creating a complete list of everything your organization owns or uses online. This includes:
- Websites and web applications
- Email systems
- Cloud services
- Social media accounts
- Mobile apps
- Any other digital resources

Asset Classification
-------------------
1. Critical Assets (High Priority)
   - Main business website
   - Customer data systems
   - Payment systems
   - Core business applications
   
   Why they're critical:
   - Essential for business operations
   - Contains sensitive information
   - Affects many users
   - High business impact if compromised

2. Important Assets (Medium Priority)
   - Marketing websites
   - Support systems
   - Development tools
   - Communication platforms
   
   Why they're important:
   - Supports business operations
   - Contains some sensitive data
   - Affects some users
   - Moderate business impact if compromised

3. Supporting Assets (Lower Priority)
   - Test environments
   - Archive systems
   - Backup systems
   - Development resources
   
   Why they're supporting:
   - Not critical for daily operations
   - Contains limited sensitive data
   - Affects few users
   - Low business impact if compromised
