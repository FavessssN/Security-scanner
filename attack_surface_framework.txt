External Attack Surface Assessment Framework
==========================================

What is an Attack Surface?
-------------------------
Think of your organization's attack surface as all the ways someone could potentially access or interact with your digital assets. This includes:
- Your website
- Email systems
- Social media accounts
- Cloud services
- Mobile apps
- Any other digital presence

Our Assessment Approach
----------------------
1. Finding Your Digital Footprint
   - Discovering all your online presence
   - Identifying websites and domains
   - Finding email systems
   - Locating cloud services
   - Mapping social media accounts

2. Checking for Security Issues
   - Looking for outdated systems
   - Finding exposed information
   - Checking for weak passwords
   - Identifying misconfigurations
   - Spotting security gaps

3. Understanding Your Risks
   - Evaluating potential threats
   - Assessing vulnerability impact
   - Identifying sensitive data
   - Understanding business impact
   - Prioritizing risks

4. Making Improvements
   - Fixing security issues
   - Updating outdated systems
   - Strengthening passwords
   - Correcting misconfigurations
   - Closing security gaps
